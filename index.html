New takes get pulled earlier by this much, so your vocals land on the beat even when Bluetooth makes you hear it late. Run a test with the same headphones you'll record with.

Clap test (best for recording)

Headphones on, tap start, then clap along to the clicks you hear for about 10 seconds. On the phone speaker, just stay quiet and let the mic hear the clicks.

Tap test

Same idea as Fortnite Festival's calibration: you hear 4 clicks to get the tempo, then tap the pad on each of the next 24, going by sound, not sight. This only measures playback lag, so the clap test is more accurate for recording.
